# Big Lever Ventures

We bring together experienced leadership, exceptional collaborators, and intelligent systems to build products and tackle demanding enterprise problems.

Our AI Collaboratory connects people across engineering, investing, product, and operations. We work through deliberate planning, rapid iteration, and rigorous review, with shared ownership in what we build.

## Enterprise work: Big Lever AI

Our flagship offering brings software intelligence to software M&A.

Big Lever AI combines a purpose-built analysis platform with experienced investor and operator judgment. It translates technical evidence into implications for price, risk, repair investment, and the software’s competitive position.

**Before acquisition:** understand software condition and what you will inherit.

**During ownership:** assess where to defend, reengineer, or exit.

[Explore Big Lever AI →](https://biglever.ai/)
